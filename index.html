  • Virtual Theremin

    An instrument in the space between your hands

    Gesture and sound, React, 2026

    Virtual Theremin brings creative coding, computer vision, and web audio into a single performance space. Hand landmarks drive sound parameters and drum triggers, while the visual scene responds to the same movements. An audio-file player lets a personal track become part of the session.

I’m Harvey, a media artist, graphic designer, and AI engineer who likes understanding how things work, then imagining what else they could do.

I was the kid with a screwdriver in his pocket, taking things apart to see what was inside. That instinct never really left. I still learn by experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and asking one more question.

Gaming first drew me toward graphic design and a future in the games industry. From there, I found my way into interactive experiences, photography, video editing, and gaming content through RayBits Gaming. As AI opened up new possibilities, I began exploring how it could bring these interests together and help turn ideas into useful tools.

Today, most of my work is AI systems and automation: tools that take repetitive work off people’s hands and give AI agents what they need to be useful. Enigma MCP, the shared memory my own agents use every day, came out of that. I’m a designer as much as an engineer, so I still design, record, and edit, and I’m still exploring cybersecurity. Each project gives me a reason to try something unfamiliar and understand it a little better.

I want technology to fit naturally into everyday life: to help us become more capable, use our time well, and make room for the things that matter. I see AI as the way to get there, and my work is about making it practical for everyday use.

Outside the screen, I sometimes pick up a guitar or write a poem. This portfolio is a growing collection of what I make, what I’m learning, and the things that keep me curious. There’s more than one side to the work, and to the person behind it.

How I work

I work as the architect and the reviewer. I define what a system must do, break it into steps that can be verified, and direct AI coding agents to build it. Nothing ships until it passes the tests and checks I set.
