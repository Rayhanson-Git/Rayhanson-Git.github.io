THE IDEA

One click.
Another center of gravity.

An interactive canvas of orbiting dots. Build new centers of motion, change the palette, and find a rhythm somewhere between stillness and speed.

Orbit Playground turns each click into a new center for an orbiting dot. The canvas starts with a small collection of orbiters and builds into overlapping trails. Color and speed controls make the same underlying system feel quiet, playful, or restless.

EXPLORE THE EXPERIENCE

A few gestures.
Room to play.

Best explored with a mouse and keyboard.

Click / hold mouse
Add an orbiter, or add them gradually.
1 / 2 / 3
Change the color palette.
↑ / ↓
Increase or decrease orbital speed.
C / R
Clear the canvas, or restore the starting scene.
